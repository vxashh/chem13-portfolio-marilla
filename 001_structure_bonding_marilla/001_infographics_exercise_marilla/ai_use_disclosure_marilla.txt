Artificial intelligence (AI) tools were used as an assisting resource in the preparation of the three organic compound infographics.

  AI was used to help generate and organize infographic content, suggest concise explanations, improve the visual layout and design, and assist in identifying relevant chemical information. The compounds covered were geosmin, capsaicin, and chlorophyll a.

  The generated information was reviewed and revised to improve scientific accuracy and alignment with the requirements of the activity. Reliable scientific references were also consulted to verify important chemical information, including molecular formulas, molar masses, structures, properties, uses, safety information, and chemical engineering connections.

  The final selection of information, organization, editing, and submission of the work were completed by the student. AI-generated content was treated as a drafting and design aid and was not accepted without review.
